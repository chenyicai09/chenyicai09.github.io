Chenyi Cai

Postdoctoral Research Fellow · National University of Singapore

I am a computational designer, researcher, and active participant in the urban environment. I investigate how urban environments can be encoded into meaningful representations, how these representations relate to urban performance and human experience, and how people and machines can collaborate to better understand and shape our cities. My work aims to translate these insights into actionable design solutions that enhance human experience and foster healthier urban environments.

My research spans four interconnected areas: urban search engines, performance-driven urban form, AI-based walkability evaluation, and preference-informed urban design interventions.

Previously, I was a postdoctoral researcher at the Singapore-ETH Centre’s Future Cities Laboratory Global. I received my PhD in Architecture from Southeast University, including a joint-PhD research stay at ETH Zürich.
